About

I'm a third-year CSE student who likes systems more than screens.

I'm a third-year Computer Science and Engineering student at the Islamic University of Technology, where most of my time goes into the parts of software that don't show up on screen — the logic, the plumbing, the parts that have to hold up under pressure. I got into competitive programming early, and it's still where I go to think in straight lines: clear problems, exact answers, no room to hide a mistake.

Lately that same instinct has pulled me toward backend development and, increasingly, cybersecurity — I like understanding not just how a system is built, but how it can be taken apart. Outside of contest rounds, I build things meant to be used: judge platforms, business tools, small simulators that make an abstract idea click. I'm still early in this, and still finding the edge of what I can do, which is most days the appeal.
